A local, privacy-first agent workspace that turns your promotional email into structured, searchable offer data. Messages are read through a read-only mail server — nothing is sent, archived, or deleted.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we build.

Can we start small?+

Yes. A focused MVP or a single workflow is a useful starting point. We define the essentials first, validate them with real use, and decide what deserves to come next.

How is AI used on my project?+

AI can support discovery, prototyping, implementation, test ideas, code review, and documentation. Its output is reviewed by a human. Tools and data handling are agreed before confidential project information is used.

What does collaboration look like?+

We agree on priorities, work in short iterations, and review working software together. Feedback informs the next cycle, with scope and trade-offs discussed openly.

Can you work with an existing system?+

Yes. We start by understanding the application, its constraints, and your goals. That helps us decide whether an integration, a focused improvement, or a larger modernization makes sense.
